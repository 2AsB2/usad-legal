Privacy policy

usad-backups by USAD Labs · Effective 15 November 2026

This policy explains what usad-backups does with access to a Google account. usad-backups is a tool USAD Labs uses to store encrypted backups of its own business systems in Google Drive.

What Google data we access

Only the drive.file permission: the files and the one folder (usad-backups) that this app created. We can't see, open or change any other file in your Google Drive. We don't access your email, contacts, calendar or profile.

How we use it

  • To create the usad-backups folder.
  • To upload one set of backup files a day.
  • To list and delete our own backup files older than 14 days.
  • To download a backup back if we need to restore a system.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.
  • We don't use it for ads, profiling or training AI models.
  • No person at USAD Labs reads the files in Drive. The backups are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) on our server before upload, and the key never goes to Google.

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where the sign-in is kept

After you allow access, Google gives our server a sign-in token. It is kept only in the server's private settings file, never in code or in any shared place, and is used only for the actions listed above.

How long data is kept

Backup files are deleted automatically after 14 days. Removing access (below) stops new uploads; files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

How to remove access

Go to Google Account › Third-party apps, choose usad-backups and remove access. You can also delete the usad-backups folder in Drive at any time.

Your rights

If your personal data is inside a backup of a USAD Labs system, you have rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), including asking what we hold about you and asking us to delete it.

Contact

Write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for usad-backups.
